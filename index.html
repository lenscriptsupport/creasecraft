Common questions

I would rather not fold my phone.

Open Settings from the level map and turn on “Use on-screen fold control”. A fold-angle slider appears during play, and every one of the 30 levels can be solved with it. It is not an easier mode; it is the same control by another route.

The hinge stopped doing anything.

Fold input pauses while the app is in the background or the puzzle is paused. Nothing is lost. Reopen the app, or switch to the on-screen control in Settings. Losing hinge input never fails a puzzle.

A puzzle reset part of itself.

That is the design. On levels with a wire, folding before the charge is full disconnects that one wire and nothing else. The rest of the puzzle, and your progress, stay as they were. There are no fail states and no timers.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone only, inside the app. There is no account and no sync. Deleting the app removes everything.

Can I replay a level I have finished?

Yes. Completed levels stay open, and replaying one never removes it from your completed list. Only one puzzle can be in progress at a time, so starting a different level asks first if it would replace an unfinished one.

Does it work with VoiceOver?

Yes. Puzzle elements, their state and your progress are available as text rather than by colour, and the fold slider reports its angle in degrees. Reduced Motion is respected throughout.
